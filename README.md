# Immersive Mercenaries

A Crusader Kings III mercenary overhaul I made to make them more meaningful (In my opinion)

---

## What This Mod Changes

Mercenary company tiers increased from three to five.

Levies are removed from mercenaries entirely instead they consist of only professional MAA and knights.

Mercenary company size scales with the cultural innovations of the culture that produces it.

Hire range has been increased compared to vanilla.

The number of knights scales with company size (Unsure if it works currently).

The number of regiments in each company progresses from one to five instead of one to three.

The cost of the MAA regiments of mercenary companies as a fraction of what those regiments cost when buying them is reduced from 50% to 30% (Subject to change)

Rulers are allowed to go into debt up to 3 years in order to hire mercenaries instead of 2.
